# Curriculum and media provenance

Independent study companion, not affiliated with UC Berkeley.

- Official course: https://data140.org/
- Textbook: Ani Adhikari and Jim Pitman, https://github.com/prob140/textbook — CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
- Fall 2026 assignments/datasets: https://github.com/data-140-berkeley/materials-fa26 — CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Exact retrieved source commit SHAs are recorded in scripts/curriculum-source.json.

25 original chapter introductions, examples, scripts and quiz explanations cover mathematical facts; they do not reproduce textbook prose or artwork and do not replace every section, proof, exercise or lab. The 175 source links index 153 textbook notebooks, 17 Fall 2026 public assignment notebooks and five data/text files available at retrieval. Source files are linked, not redistributed as adapted course notes.

The included MP4s are original programmed animations, rendered using Pillow/FFmpeg, and contain visual captions without embedded narration. Optional Listen uses browser speech synthesis. Chapters 1, 2 and 4 also link to approved unlisted Descript-hosted original vector animations with AI voice, captions and stock music. Their visual timing was not verified. These are not generated cinematic AI video footage.

This source package includes no live video generation, third-party account credentials, or unpublished/authenticated university material. The separate cinematic fruit frame previously generated in chat is not used as animation in this release.
